Live tools that put apprenticeship opportunities in front of students while giving business owners a direct, accountable pipeline of candidates.

The problem

Too many students never hear about apprenticeships until it is too late

Employers who offer apprenticeships struggle to reach the people who would benefit most. The routes that already exist list them alongside every other role, and students only hear about the opportunity after the window has closed.

The fix

A dedicated marketplace where students and business owners connect directly

We give students clear, early visibility of real opportunities and give business owners a direct route to motivated candidates. The platform is live, and we are now scaling the tools on both sides of the match.

How it works

  1. 1

    Discover

    Students find apprenticeship opportunities matched to their interests and region through a curated feed.

  2. 2

    Match

    Employers receive student profiles ranked by intent and relevance, then shortlist candidates.

  3. 3

    Connect

    Both parties communicate directly through the platform to begin the application conversation.

  4. 4

    Track

    Employers monitor listing performance and adjust in real time based on first-party analytics.

What the platform does

  1. 01

    Apprenticeship discovery feed

    Students browse apprenticeship openings curated by location, sector, and skill level. Each listing links directly to the employer's application process with no intermediary. Business owners control their own listings through a self-serve dashboard.

  2. 02

    Employer matching tools

    Business owners receive matched student profiles based on stated interests, qualifications, and location. The matching engine prioritises intent signals — saved listings, sector preferences — over passive browsing data. Employers can shortlist and initiate contact through the platform.

  3. 03

    Student profile builder

    Students create structured profiles that surface their strengths to relevant employers without requiring a traditional CV upload. The builder guides them through qualifications, interests, and career goals in plain language. Profiles remain editable and portable across applications.

  4. 04

    Analytics for employers

    Employers see views, saves, and application starts for each listing in real time. The dashboard benchmarks performance against sector averages so owners can adjust timing or descriptions. All data is first-party and visible only to the listing owner.

Why this is being built, and why now

The seed of UCAS was planted years ago, observing firsthand the frustration both students and employers faced. I saw bright, motivated young people unaware of the incredible pathways apprenticeships offered, and committed businesses struggling to connect with the very talent they desperately needed. It was clear the existing systems were failing. This isn't just about a market gap; it's about unlocking potential and fostering the next generation of skilled professionals. We built UCAS to bridge that divide, driven by a belief that the right connections can transform futures on both sides. The platform is live, and we are now scaling the tools on both sides of the match to fulfil that vision.

Questions investors ask

Is the platform live or still in development?

The marketplace is live. Students and employers are actively registering, and we are now scaling the tools on both sides based on early usage data.

How do you make money?

Employers pay to list and promote apprenticeship opportunities. Students use the platform at no cost. We are exploring premium employer tools as the next revenue layer.

What is the total addressable market?

There are over 200,000 apprenticeship starts in the UK each year, and employer demand consistently outpaces student awareness. Our target is the gap between those two figures.

Who are your competitors?

Existing job boards list apprenticeships alongside every other role. Government platforms exist but lack employer-side tooling. No one has built a dedicated marketplace connecting students and business owners directly.

What traction do you have?

The platform is live with employers and students actively using it. We share specific metrics and growth data in investor conversations.

How do you acquire students?

We work through schools, colleges, and careers services that already have student attention. The platform integrates with existing advisory workflows rather than competing with them.

What does the capital go toward?

Engineering to expand employer-side tooling, partnerships with careers services for student acquisition, and data infrastructure to improve matching accuracy.

What is the long-term vision?

To become the default place where UK students first encounter apprenticeships and where employers first look for apprentices — replacing fragmented job boards and government portals.
